# P0-15 — Proof-of-Work badge and receipt

**বাংলা সারাংশ:**
- প্রতিটি শেষ-হওয়া turn-এর নিচে **Proof-of-Work ব্যাজ**: `✓ ৪টা check পাস · ১৪ লাইন বদলেছে · ollama:qwen3.5:9b (2) + deepseek:deepseek-chat (1)`। **শুধু যা মাপা হয়েছে** তাই দেখায় — যে LSP/reuse এখনো মাপা হয় না তার "০" বানিয়ে দেখায় না; Verify না চললে লেখে "যাচাই হয়নি"; ব্যর্থ হলে লাল ✗।
- **রসিদ** (ব্যাজের "Receipt" বোতামে): প্রতি মডেল-সারিতে ধাপ, সময়, token, খরচ, এবং ধাপ "এই কম্পিউটারে / Rotation Chain / আপনার মডেল"-এ চলেছে কি না; শেষে মোট ও "২টা লোকাল · ১টা chain"। দাম-ছাড়া মডেলে "no price" (বানানো $0.00 নয়)।
- রসিদ সম্পূর্ণ **Step Trace-এর সারি থেকে গণনা** (`trace::receipt`, RPC `turn.receipt`), তাই trace-এর সাথে অমিল অসম্ভব; test স্বাধীনভাবে ওই সারিগুলোর যোগফলের সাথে মেলায়।
- English + বাংলা; TrustChip/CostMeter-এর একই রং/টোকেন।

## What changed
`trace.rs::receipt` + RPC `turn.receipt` (+ protocol `ReceiptRow`/`ReceiptTotals`); app: `kernel/ProofBadge.tsx`, `kernel/proofFacts.ts`, `TurnFooter`/`TurnStream` (badge under the footer; lines from the turn's changed files), `TurnVerifyData` gains `checksPassed/checksFailed`, strings en + bn; `scripts/visual-bundle.mjs` (component fixtures + stable-shot rule).

## Tests
- Rust: receipt totals == independent sum of the Step Trace rows (steps, tokens, time, cost, local 2 / chain 1), the other turn excluded, one row per model+purpose; unpriced model -> `null` cost; empty turn -> empty receipt.
- App (vitest, 6 new): badge parts show only the measured (4 cases incl. failed and running), models line, markup of badge + receipt with totals = sum of rows, closed vs open, failure drawn as failure, Bengali.
- **Visual regression:** the unit test renders the real component to `tests/visual/fixtures/proof-badge.html`; `smoke:visual` photographs it in dark and light (`proof-badge-dark/light`, 0.000% on re-run). Checked by eye: closed, open with receipt, failed.
- Whole app: typecheck, eslint (0 warnings), vitest 263, `protocol:check` 183 methods, visual 6/6 stable on 5 consecutive runs, a11y 0.

## Problems found on the way
- The visual check was flaky once more (a light-theme shot caught the onboarding dialog half faded). Fixed by a rule rather than a tolerance: a shot counts only when a second shot of the same build agrees; otherwise "UNSTABLE" is reported.
- Two lint warnings (fast refresh) from exporting helpers beside a component: moved to `proofFacts.ts`.

## Not done / honest limits
- "LSP errors" and "reused functions" are not on the badge: nothing measures them until P1-12 and P3-1; the badge omits them rather than print zeros.
- Check counts come from Verify's `checks` (tests, build, lint, typecheck); a project with none shows "not verified".
- The badge reads `turn.receipt` after the turn; a turn with no traced step (the switch off, or a CLI engine whose model calls SDC does not see) has a badge without a receipt button.
- The visual check of the badge is the component in isolation; its placement inside a live turn is not photographed (no backend-free path to a finished turn).
